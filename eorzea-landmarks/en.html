Eorzea Landmarks

EoLan

An unofficial companion app for FINAL FANTASY XIV. See at a glance which Sightseeing Log spots you can reach right now, based on the current Eorzean Time and weather.

When you would use it

Find a spot you can visit right now

Keep it open on your phone or tablet while you play on PC. Spots whose time and weather conditions are met right now appear at the top of the list.

Stop missing rare conditions

Set a reminder bell on spots that only appear under uncommon conditions. You get a notification when the conditions line up, so you do not have to watch the sky.

Fill in the spots you cannot find

Play a route video without leaving the app, and save your own notes and video links so you know where to go next time.

Features

Live Eorzean Time and weather

The current Eorzean Time and weather are shown in real time, so you can immediately see which unvisited spots are available.

Track your Sightseeing Log progress

Check off each spot as you visit it and see your overall progress at a glance.

Route videos and personal notes

Play route videos (YouTube and Niconico) inside the app, and edit or save your own notes and video links.

Filter and search

Narrow spots down by expansion, area, weather, time of day and keyword — in any combination.

Weather across every area

Check the current weather for all 72 areas at once. Pick an area to see a countdown to the next change and the forecast ahead.

Upcoming appearance times

For spots with irregular conditions, see when they will next be available over the coming 48 hours.

Built for tablets too

On tablets the app switches to a two-column layout so you can see the list and the details side by side.

Free, with no ads

There are no ads and no in-app purchases. Supporting development through Ko-fi is entirely optional.

Your data stays on your device

Your checkmarks and notes are stored only on your device and are never sent anywhere. Export and import lets you move them yourself when you change devices.

How to join the Android closed test

Google Play requires a period of closed testing before an app can be released publicly. We are looking for people willing to help test the Android version.

What you need

  • An Android device
  • A Google account signed in to Google Play

Steps

  1. Join the Google Group using the button below. Members of that group are registered as testers.
  2. Open the opt-in link and choose "Become a tester".
  3. Install the app from Google Play.
1. Join the Google Group 2. Open the opt-in link

About your email address

You are registered as a tester simply by joining the Google Group. The developer does not collect or store your email address separately through a form or anywhere else. It is used only to distribute the closed test, never for marketing or any other purpose. The group will be deleted once testing is finished.

One request

Google requires at least 12 testers to stay opted in continuously for 14 days before the app can be published. If you can leave the app installed and stay opted in for those 14 days, it would be a real help.

Feedback and bug reports are always welcome through the contact form.

About the developer

Eorzea Landmarks is an unofficial, fan-made tool built by Crystal Works, an individual developer. If you are deciding whether to install it, here are the facts worth knowing.

  • The iOS version is already published on the App Store. This is the Android version of that same app, which has passed Apple's review process (view on the App Store)
  • No account or sign-in is required
  • No ads and no in-app purchases
  • Your checkmarks and notes are stored only on your device, never sent to a server
  • No advertising identifier (IDFA) is used
  • Only anonymous usage data (screen views, device type, OS, language) and crash reports are collected through Firebase, to find and fix problems

See the privacy policy for details, or get in touch through the contact form.
